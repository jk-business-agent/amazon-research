Methodology & Sources

Research was conducted on August 10, 2026 using WebSearch and WebFetch exclusively (no paid API). Amazon.com pages (Best Sellers, Movers & Shakers, category zgbs pages) were inaccessible due to network egress proxy restrictions — this is the same condition as every prior report in this archive. All 20 products were discovered and verified through secondary sources: retail technology press, consumer publications, Amazon seller analytics platforms, and deal-tracking sites. WebFetch was blocked on nearly all commercial domains; product data was drawn primarily from WebSearch result snippets, which provide accurate article titles, headlines, and quoted figures but are summaries rather than full fetched pages. Accordingly, all specific numbers in this report (prices, discount percentages, monthly order volumes) should be treated as approximate and confirmed only to the extent that they appeared in search result snippet text.

Verification rule: VERIFIED = at least 2 independent sources confirmed the product's current trending or best-seller status. LOW-CONFIDENCE = only one source found, or only category-level (not product-level) confirmation. 6 of 20 products are LOW-CONFIDENCE, most of them long-running RECURRING items carried forward on category strength and prior-report history rather than fresh August 10 sourcing. No VERIFIED label was applied to any product based on fewer than 2 independent confirmations.

Anti-fabrication note: No specific sales-rank numbers, units-sold figures, or revenue figures from Amazon's own systems were available or cited (Amazon pages were inaccessible). The figure "40,000–60,000 monthly orders" cited for COSRX derives from a search result snippet referencing ASINsight's 2026 data — the underlying ASINsight page was blocked; this figure is approximate. No figures were invented or averaged across conflicting sources.

Rotation note: Today's category rotation deprioritized tools & home improvement, baby, and toys & games (all covered in recent prior reports) in favor of Beauty, Apparel, Health & Household, and Grocery to maintain cross-report coverage diversity. Back-to-school timing made Office Products impossible to deprioritize.
