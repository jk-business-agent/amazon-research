Executive Summary

The week of October 22, 2026 — nine days before Halloween — shows a clear bifurcation in Amazon's best-seller landscape: seasonal urgency (Halloween costumes, heated blankets, immune supplements) is colliding with year-round structural trends (protein nutrition, K-beauty, creatine) in ways that reveal how broadly the American consumer's baseline has shifted. The standout new entrant this week is the Bedsure GentleSoft Electric Heated Blanket, which earned Amazon's #1 Best Seller badge in Electric Blankets as fall temperatures arrived nationwide; it was featured prominently in post-Prime Big Deal Days deal coverage and carries reported sales of over 5,000 units in a single month. The Fairlife Core Power Elite 42g Chocolate Protein Shake also enters the tracked list as a newly confirmed top seller — third-party analytics estimates it at approximately 100,000 monthly units sold, surpassing the longer-tracked Premier Protein in raw unit volume at a higher protein content. Meanwhile, The Let Them Theory by Mel Robbins continues to top Amazon's overall books chart more than a year after release, a rare feat that reflects the sustained cultural resonance of the mindset/self-help genre. Pre-holiday momentum is building in Electronics (AirPods Pro 3, Kindle 2026, Echo Dot), and the Inflatable T-Rex costume enters its Halloween peak week for the third consecutive report. Note: Amazon.com direct web access was DNS-blocked during this research run; all findings are drawn from secondary press, deal roundups, and third-party analytics — see methodology section.

Methodology & Sources

Research approach: This report used WebSearch and WebFetch exclusively (no paid API). Amazon.com was DNS-inaccessible from this research environment (as has been the case in all recent runs); all product data was sourced from secondary press, deal roundups, and third-party Amazon analytics tools.

Verification standard: VERIFIED = corroborated by at least 2 independent sources; LOW-CONFIDENCE = 1 source, or primary evidence is recurring history + one indirect reference. No product was silently upgraded from LOW-CONFIDENCE to VERIFIED.

Anti-fabrication note: All specific numbers cited in this report (unit sales estimates, prices, discount percentages, review counts) were read directly from the fetched source content or search result snippets. No figures were averaged, inferred from context, or reconstructed from memory.

Degraded-mode fallback: Amazon.com Movers & Shakers and Best Sellers pages were inaccessible (DNS block). Amazon New Releases pages were not checked.
