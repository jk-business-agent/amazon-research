Methodology & Sources

Research date: July 27, 2026. Sibling report processed: July 27, 2026 (Amazon Top Sellers). Electronics excluded: Beats Solo 4, Apple Watch Series 11, Amazon Echo Dot 5th Gen, Apple AirPods Pro 3, Samsung Galaxy Tab A11+ (5 products).

VERIFIED status requires two independent corroborating sources for American manufacturing or ownership — e.g., brand about page plus independent press naming the facility, certification body listing, or public records. LOW-CONFIDENCE means only the brand's own marketing copy supports the American-made claim, or where manufacturing origin is simply unknown.

Sources consulted (97 total): Brand about pages and product pages; independent press (local newspapers, trade publications, Bloomberg, WSJ); FDA supplement facility databases; NSF International certification search; Labdoor product testing reports; Examine.com ingredient research; SEC and state business registration filings; PETA Beauty Without Bunnies database; EWG Skin Deep; affiliate network listings (Impact, ShareASale, Awin, CJ, Rakuten); AVMA/FDA pet food recall databases; FTC Made in USA labeling guidance.

Display order: NEW products before RECURRING; within each group, in-band ($25–$100) products listed before out-of-band; higher repurchase-frequency products prioritized within same band.
