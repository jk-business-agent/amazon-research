Verification protocol: VERIFIED = corroborated by at least 2 independent source URLs (search snippets with specific product names, prices, or editorial claims count as source evidence when they reference distinct publications). LOW-CONFIDENCE = only search-snippet-level evidence from sources that could not be directly fetched, or where specific product-level data was not directly read from page content. Recurring products without new explicit source confirmation rely on prior verified session history.

Anti-fabrication note: No specific sales-rank numbers, units-sold figures, or revenue figures are cited in this report unless they appeared explicitly in a search snippet attributed to a specific source. The Cortisol Manager entry's "30,000 monthly sales" figure came from an asinsight.com search snippet; the page itself was inaccessible, and the figure is flagged LOW-CONFIDENCE accordingly. Growth outlooks are qualitative estimates, not quantitative forecasts.
