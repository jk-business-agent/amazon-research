Methodology & Sources

Verification standard: VERIFIED status requires at least two independent public sources confirming US manufacturing or production (not merely US headquarters or brand ownership). LOW-CONFIDENCE indicates US headquarters confirmed, but domestic manufacturing could not be independently verified from a second source.

Electronics exclusion: Per workflow design, all Electronics products from the sibling agent's report are excluded. 4 Electronics products were excluded from the September 3, 2026 sibling report, leaving 18 non-Electronics products researched.

Circuit-breaker: Per-product research halts after 3 source failures yielding 0 verifiable alternatives. This report's circuit-breaker: Ninja Crispi Glass Air Fryer (confirmed structural gap — no US consumer air fryer manufacturer).

Structural gaps tracked: (1) Vacuum-insulated stainless steel water bottles — Liberty Bottleworks closed June 2026; no US stainless vacuum-insulated bottle manufacturer confirmed. (2) Consumer air fryers — entirely Asia-manufactured. (3) Hydrocolloid acne patches — Korean manufacturing industry standard; no US manufacturer confirmed in 4 research cycles. (4) Snail secretion filtrate — no US commercial producer identified.

Affiliate data: Rates sourced from brand affiliate program pages, ShareASale/Awin/FlexOffers/Impact Radius network directories, and influencer partnership pages. Rates are approximate and subject to change. Verify current terms before promotion.

Price data: Amazon list prices observed at research time. Prices fluctuate; shown as approximate bands only.

Sources: Approximately 85 sources checked across brand websites, SEC filings, facility announcements, press releases, third-party review sites (Examine.com, EWG, NSF International), affiliate network directories, and business news sources. Individual source citations available on request.
