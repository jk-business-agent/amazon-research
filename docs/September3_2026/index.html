Methodology & Sources

Research was conducted entirely via WebSearch-derived snippets on September 3, 2026. Direct WebFetch access to Amazon.com (Movers & Shakers, Best Sellers, and New Releases pages) was blocked (EGRESS_BLOCKED) on all attempts. Direct access to most editorial, analytics, and aggregator sites was also blocked, including: 9to5mac.com, androidcentral.com, techtimes.com, popsci.com, finance.yahoo.com, huffpost.com, garagegymreviews.com, today.com, aol.com, amzscout.net, shelftrend.com, smartscout.com, retaildive.com, modernretail.co, hunker.com, parade.com, buzzfeed.com, and approximately 15 additional domains. In all cases, these sites' search snippets remained accessible via the WebSearch tool and served as the primary data source. This fallback is documented per the workflow specification.

Verification standard: VERIFIED = product corroborated by snippets from at least 2 distinct source domains across separate WebSearch queries. LOW-CONFIDENCE = only a single source domain could be identified. No specific numerical claims (exact sales ranks, unit counts, revenue figures, or precise discount percentages) were included unless they appeared verbatim in a retrieved snippet. Where sources disagreed — notably on the Sony WH-1000XM5 discount percentage — a verbal description was substituted rather than picking one figure.

Source domains consulted (23 total): 9to5mac.com, androidcentral.com, popsci.com, macrumors.com, theverge.com, allure.com, byrdie.com, sephora.com, healthline.com, examine.com, garagegymreviews.com, chewy.com, petmd.com, businessinsider.com, today.com, buzzfeed.com, rtings.com, consumerreports.org, retaildive.com, modernretail.co, usatoday.com, hunker.com, 9to5toys.com.
