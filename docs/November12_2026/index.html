Executive Summary

November 12 marks the opening of Amazon's most aggressive pre-holiday sales push in years, with early Black Friday deals driving record-low prices across electronics, smart home, and streaming categories one full week before the official November 19 event launch. The standout headline: Ring Indoor Cam 2K at $24.99 (down from ~$60) and Apple AirTag 2nd Generation 4-pack at an all-time low $69.99 are both carrying Amazon #1 Bestseller or Best Seller badges, confirming that price cuts are translating directly into purchase velocity — consumers are buying now, not waiting for Black Friday. In toys, two strong new entrants emerge: the Primal Hatch Megalodon Hatching Shark Egg is 2026's viral breakout toy (on Amazon's official Top 100+ Toys list), and Magna-Tiles continues its perennial holiday STEM dominance with the Undersea Adventure set. The broader economic signal is notable: front-loaded holiday spending, now arriving in the second week of November rather than the fourth, reflects both Amazon's deliberate staged-deal strategy and consumers' growing willingness to act early to secure inventory on viral and limited items. Methodology & Sources

Research method: This report was produced exclusively through web search (WebSearch tool) and attempted page fetches. Amazon.com and most major retail press outlets (washingtontimes.com, popsci.com, 9to5toys.com, shopping.yahoo.com) were inaccessible due to DNS restrictions in this run's network environment — this is a fully degraded-mode run. All product data was gathered from search-result snippets, secondary press, and third-party analytics platforms.

Verification rule: VERIFIED items were corroborated by at least 2 independent sources (Amazon's own platform data via snippets, plus at least one independent news outlet or analytics platform). LOW-CONFIDENCE items had only 1 source or general category references and are marked visibly. No specific sales figures, rankings, or percentages were included in VERIFIED cards unless they appeared as direct text in a cited source's search snippet — no figures were reconstructed from memory or general context.

Fallback noted: Amazon Movers & Shakers and Amazon Best Sellers pages (amazon.com) were inaccessible. Amazon New Releases pages were also inaccessible. Product discovery relied on early Black Friday deal coverage (androidpolice.com, today.com, cordcuttersnews.com, sporked.com, appleinsider.com, androidauthority.com, the-gadgeteer.com, pressbee.net, security.org, fox21news.com, dealsfinders.com), Amazon's own holiday press (aboutamazon.com via snippet), toy holiday roundups (today.com, forbes.com, nayafoundit.com), and third-party seller analytics (amzscout.net, amzprep.com, asinsight.com, metricscart.com, similarweb.com).
