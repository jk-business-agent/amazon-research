Today marks the final day of Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days 2026 (Oct 6–7, wrapping overnight Oct 7–8), making this the most deal-intensive research day in the series. Electronics led with 8 of 18 products — spanning Amazon's own device lineup (Echo Dot, Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Kindle Paperwhite) through premium consumer tech (Apple AirPods Pro 3, Ring doorbell cameras, Blink floodlights) — as expected during Amazon's flagship fall sale event. INABA Churu Cat Treats logs its 21st report appearance. Methodology & Sources

Research approach: All data gathered via WebSearch and WebFetch on October 8, 2026. Amazon.com was inaccessible via WebFetch (DNS resolution failure on all direct amazon.com attempts — same network restriction observed in prior runs). Additionally, the following major outlets were inaccessible via WebFetch: nbcnews.com, rollingstone.com, techradar.com, tasteofhome.com, thedailybeast.com, wwd.com, hollywoodreporter.com, today.com, popsci.com, womanandhome.com, fitandwell.com, chatelaine.com, elevenforum.com, goodreads.com, northcarolinacharm.com, shopback.com, merca20.com, sellerapp.com, iheartdogs.com, and others. All data for blocked domains was obtained via Google Search snippet text returned in WebSearch results — these snippets provided quoted prices, product names, and claims that appeared to be from the articles. All such data should be treated as snippet-level confidence rather than full-article corroboration.

Verification rule: VERIFIED = corroborated by at least 2 independent source domains. LOW-CONFIDENCE = only one domain confirmed for October 2026. INABA Churu Cat Treats is LOW-CONFIDENCE for today's specific date because Numerator data confirmed "pet treats" as a category leader, not INABA specifically.

Event context: Prime Big Deal Days 2026 ran October 6–7 (Amazon's official dates per aboutamazon.com), with deals wrapping late October 7/early October 8. This made today's report heavily influenced by sale-event dynamics — product velocities and prices reflected during the event, not standard organic bestseller conditions. Some sources listed slightly different event dates (Oct 8–9 or Oct 10–11) that appear to be stale copy from prior years or different regions.

Distinct source domains referenced today (18): nbcnews.com, rollingstone.com, aboutamazon.com, techradar.com, tasteofhome.com, wwlp.com, tomsguide.com, thedailybeast.com, wwd.com, purewow.com, whowhatwear.com, asinsight.com, grocerydive.com, peggyomara.com, fitandwell.com, digitechbytes.com, pwice.com, yahoo.com / bestreviews.com.

(2) Medicube Jelly Cream is logged as a separate product key from Medicube Zero Pore Pads since they are distinct product SKUs; both are trending under the Medicube brand umbrella.
