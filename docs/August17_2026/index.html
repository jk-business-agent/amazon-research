Methodology & Sources

Source types checked today: Amazon best-seller/movers-and-shakers category pages (all blocked at the network egress proxy), press/editorial coverage (CNN Underscored, NBC Selected, Yahoo, Gizmodo, PCGuide, Tom's Guide, AppleInsider, MacRumors, ABC News — all blocked at proxy), industry/trade reporting (SmartScout, SupplysideSJ, Beautymatter, ProteinCompared, Business of Fashion, Cosmetics Business, PR Newswire, GarageGymReviews, CreatineFinders, ASINSight — most blocked at proxy), 9to5Toys, Consequence, SlickDeals price aggregators, and general WebSearch snippet coverage. Verification rule: each VERIFIED product has at least two independent sources cited in its card that describe the same trend/rank/price signal.

Degraded-mode note: The network egress proxy blocked every WebFetch attempt today — Amazon.com, CNN, NBC News, Yahoo, Tom's Guide, MacRumors, AppleInsider, 9to5toys, Gizmodo, Business of Fashion, and roughly two dozen other domains all returned EGRESS_BLOCKED. This report is therefore built entirely from WebSearch result snippets rather than full-page reads. Where a specific number (unit count, review count, dollar figure, discount percentage) appears in a card, it is copied from a WebSearch snippet in which that number was directly quoted — no numbers were reconstructed from memory or inferred from the surrounding context. Some product-level nuances (exact current sales rank, ASIN-level revenue) that a direct Amazon read would provide are not available in today's report.

Judgment call: The Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer and Nintendo Switch 2 accessory sourcing both rely on the Amazon.com Best Sellers category page URL surfacing as the top result in WebSearch — I've treated the fact that Amazon itself surfaces those SKUs in the "Best Sellers: Wearable Tech Glasses" and "Best Sellers: Nintendo Switch 2 Accessories" URLs as evidence of bestseller status. Corroborated in each case with one industry source (ASINSight for Ray-Ban, Tech-Insider for Switch 2 hardware numbers).
