Methodology & Sources

Sibling report processed: August 27, 2026 (14 Amazon products, 9 categories). Electronics excluded: Oura Ring 5 and Anker Prime Power Bank (hard-exclude rule). 12 non-Electronics products researched via 4 parallel general-purpose subagents, each covering 3 products.

Sort precedence applied (Step 2): (1) This agent's own NEW products before RECURRING — NEW determined by this agent's 14-day history, independent of the sibling's own NEW/RECURRING tags. (2) Within each group, products in the $25–$100 price band before outside-band products (display order only; no product excluded for price). (3) Within each group, higher repurchase-interest before lower, per per-product subagent judgment. Within tied cells, order follows natural research sequence.

Verification rule for American-ness: VERIFIED requires two independent sources — the business's own claim plus one of: a certification-body listing, independent press naming the manufacturing location, or a public-records signal (state incorporation, real factory address, USPTO/SEC filing). A claim backed only by the business's own About page or marketing copy is marked LOW-CONFIDENCE and labeled visibly; never silently upgraded.

Per-product alternative cap: Soft cap of 2–3 alternatives per product was applied. No product's circuit breaker (3 source failures with zero alternatives) was triggered. All 12 products received at least 2 alternatives.

Category gaps documented: (a) COSRX Snail 96 Mucin — snail secretion filtrate essence is not manufactured by any American brand; alternatives target the same skincare goals via HA-based and enzyme-based mechanisms, transparently noted.
