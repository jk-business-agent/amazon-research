Executive Summary

August 27 sits at the tail end of the back-to-school window and the cusp of Labor Day weekend (September 5–7) — a moment when consumer intent is simultaneously closing out summer and opening fall. The day's headline story is the creatine supplement surge, which SmartScout's Amazon marketplace analysis (published August 24–25 and picked up by multiple regional TV newsrooms) confirmed is the category's best year in three decades: up 90% year-over-year to $57.5 million per month, with women identified as the primary new-buyer demographic. That surge is lifting both traditional creatine monohydrate (Nutricost, a recurring archive fixture) and a faster-growing gummies format (Create Creatine Gummies, a new entrant today). The second major story is the continued consolidation of Korean beauty on Amazon: Medicube's Kojic Acid Turmeric Serum enters for the first time with confirmed data showing over 100,000 units per month, and its brand now holds 17 of the top 100 trending TikTok Shop skincare products in the U.S. The Oura Ring 5 — launched June 4 at $399 — makes its debut in this archive; the smart ring category grew more than 25% year-on-year (Bloomberg), and Oura holds undisputed leadership. JanSport makes its 16th consecutive appearance, likely its final days of peak back-to-school demand before a post-Labor Day seasonal dip.
