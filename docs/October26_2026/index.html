Methodology & Sources

Data collection: This run relied entirely on WebSearch-derived data (search engine snippets and secondary press coverage). Amazon.com Movers & Shakers, Best Sellers, and all Amazon product pages were inaccessible due to DNS failure on every attempt. Major editorial press sites (NBCNews.com, CNN.com, TODAY.com, HuffPost.com, TheStreet.com) also failed DNS resolution when fetched directly; their content was accessed only as text snippets from search engine results. All direct WebFetch calls to third-party analytics sites (AMZScout, ASINsight, BetterReport) also failed DNS resolution.

Verification rule: VERIFIED = corroborated by at least 2 independent sources. Because Amazon's own pages were unavailable, two distinct secondary sources (different publications or different analytics reports) were required for a VERIFIED rating. LOW-CONFIDENCE = only one source found, or sources consist of history-file prior appearances without a fresh external corroboration. LOW-CONFIDENCE items are labeled visibly in each card and were not silently upgraded.

Data recency: NBC News Prime Day bestseller roundups are approximately 4–19 days old as of today (published Oct 7–22). AMZScout tracker data is from October 12, 2026. ASINsight reports are from June–July 2026 (3–4 months old) and used for category-level corroboration, not live ranking. History-file data reflects this project's own verified findings from prior runs.

Anti-fabrication note: All specific figures cited in cards (178,963 units, 168,979 units, ~90,000 units, ~100,000 units, ~20,000 units, $234, +62%) were confirmed as appearing verbatim in the search result snippet for the cited source before inclusion. Figures that sounded plausible but could not be located in the actual snippet text were omitted and replaced with "price not confirmed" or similar language.
