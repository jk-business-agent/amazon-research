Approximately 18 distinct search queries were executed covering Amazon Movers & Shakers (via secondary press), Best Sellers across Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Health & Household, Beauty & Personal Care, Grocery, Apparel, Sports & Outdoors, Toys & Games, Pet Supplies, Baby, and Tools & Home Improvement categories. Verification standard: VERIFIED = corroborated by at least 2 independent source domains (Amazon's own Best Sellers category pages count as one source; a news article, deal aggregator, or second Amazon category page counts as a second). LOW-CONFIDENCE = only one source found, or full article inaccessible. Confidence labels were never silently upgraded to make the report look cleaner.
