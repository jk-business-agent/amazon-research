Methodology & Sources

Research approach: 15 non-Electronics products from the sibling agent's August 24, 2026 Amazon report were identified and researched using parallel general-purpose research subagents (one per 2–3 products). Electronics (Samsung Galaxy Buds 4 Pro, Apple AirPods 4, Apple AirPods Pro 3) were excluded per standing workflow rules. All American business alternatives were found via WebSearch and WebFetch across approximately 92 distinct source fetches spanning company websites, independent press, certification databases, affiliate network listings, and sports science databases.

Verification standard: VERIFIED = at least two independent sources confirm US manufacturing or assembly, where at least one source is not the brand's own marketing (e.g., independent press, certification body, retailer database, trade record). LOW-CONFIDENCE = only a single source found, or all confirming sources are the brand's own marketing materials without independent corroboration. 17 VERIFIED, 10 LOW-CONFIDENCE across 27 alternatives.

Ownership caveats: Two VERIFIED alternatives carry foreign ownership: (1) Thayers Natural Remedies — US manufacturing in Easton, CT is VERIFIED, but L'Oréal (France) acquired the brand in 2020; it is not an independent American business. (2) Paula's Choice — Unilever (Netherlands) acquired in 2021, plus a 2025 class action raises product claims questions; rated LOW-CONFIDENCE on manufacturing origin. These are noted transparently throughout the report.

Structural category gaps confirmed this report: (1) Robot vacuums — iRobot (now Chinese-owned per December 2025 Picea Robotics acquisition) is removed from American recommendations; Matic Robots is the sole US-assembled option, LOW-CONFIDENCE. (2) Vacuum-insulated stainless water bottles — Liberty Bottleworks (Liberty Lake, WA) permanently closed June 2026; Polar Bottle (foam insulation) remains the sole VERIFIED domestic option. (3) K-beauty PDRN hydrogel eye patches — manufacturing technology is overwhelmingly South Korean; Peter Thomas Roth provides a comparable but technically different US alternative. (4) Energy drinks — no major-scale VERIFIED US-manufactured functional energy drink brand found; ECHELON USA and Bloom Nutrition rated LOW-CONFIDENCE.
