Executive Summary

August 13 lands in peak back-to-school season and the sustained tail of a K-beauty summer supercycle — two forces that define today's headline findings. The K-beauty wave remains the most structurally significant story in Amazon retail: Medicube Zero Pore Pad 2.0 holds the #1 Beauty & Personal Care ranking for a second consecutive quarter, Biodance Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask enters this report for the first time at Q2's #3 beauty position (over 300 million masks sold globally), and COSRX secured multiple top-5 Prime Day rankings — together, K-beauty brands now account for an estimated 10.5% of all Amazon beauty searches, up from 6.5% in 2025. On the back-to-school front, NIIMBOT's new D110 Bluetooth label maker is rising fast on the Office Products new-releases chart, joining the now-familiar seasonal surge of JanSport backpacks, Mead composition books, and Crayola supplies. Perhaps the most notable category shift is in hydration wellness: LMNT Zero Sugar Electrolytes debuts as a new entrant holding the #1 position in Amazon's Sports Nutrition Electrolyte Replacement Drinks — a sign that high-sodium, athlete-focused supplements are crossing into mass-market grocery shopping. The ECOVACS DEEBOT T90 PRO Omni (Amazon's current best-selling robot vacuum at $699.99) represents the premium smart-home appliance market showing continued strength. Methodology & Sources

Research approach: This report followed the standard workflow: (1) attempt direct Amazon page fetches (Movers & Shakers, Best Sellers, New Releases), (2) secondary WebSearch for industry/press coverage, (3) attempt WebFetch on individual editorial URLs, (4) compile and verify findings.

Degraded access — full session: Amazon.com was fully blocked by the network egress proxy (EGRESS_BLOCKED on all attempts). Additionally, the vast majority of secondary editorial URLs — including Modern Retail, BeautyMatter, BGR, Today.com, TechRadar, NBC News, ABC News, PRNewswire, wiseppc.com, and many others — were also blocked at the network level. No WebFetch call returned content this session. All product data and specific figures in this report are sourced exclusively from search-result snippets (text excerpts surfaced by the search engine from those articles). This means specific numbers (ranks, revenue estimates, search-volume figures) were read from search result excerpts and are cited with their source URLs, but the full article text was not independently read page-by-page.

Verification rule: VERIFIED = corroborated by at least 2 independent sources (each identified in the card's source list). LOW-CONFIDENCE = only one source found, or the sole source is a brand's own press release, or the claim could not be confirmed in today's degraded research environment. No LOW-CONFIDENCE labels were silently removed.

Anti-fabrication note: All specific figures cited (e.g., Medicube's 13.23% Prime Day beauty sales share; 4.3M searches; INABA Churu $1,858,594 monthly revenue; Owala 20,000 monthly sales; LMNT #1 Electrolyte ranking) were read from search result snippet text. None were invented or inferred from general category context. Where a specific number was unavailable, the card states "price not confirmed" or describes the claim in qualitative terms.
