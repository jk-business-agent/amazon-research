Executive Summary

Apple's "Surprise and Shine" event on September 9 is the single biggest force shaping Amazon's charts today: AirPods 5 pre-orders went live immediately after the event at a $129 starting price that now includes Active Noise Cancellation — a feature that cost $179 with the previous generation — and Apple Watch Series 12 pre-orders are also active, with both products shipping September 18. The resulting accessory cycle will influence Amazon's electronics rankings for weeks. Away from Apple, creatine is having its largest year in three decades: per a SmartScout analysis of Amazon marketplace data through August 2026, the category hit $721 million in trailing 12-month revenue with a 90% year-over-year surge, fueled by a major demographic shift as women now drive a substantial share of demand. Fall seasonality is asserting itself across multiple categories simultaneously — cropped puffer vests are moving fast in Apparel, elderberry gummies are climbing for immunity-prep, COSRX's peptide serum is the new K-beauty standout displacing snail mucin as the lead trend driver, and Toniebox 2's screen-free audio platform is on Amazon's own "Toys We Love 2026" list with North America sales up 36% year-over-year. For an audience watching American economic growth, the Apple product cycle's power to instantly reorganize consumer purchasing across multiple categories — and the creatine market's remarkable demographic expansion — are the two headline business stories of the day.

Methodology & Sources

Research approach: This report was produced via WebSearch and WebFetch only (no paid API), per the workflow specification. Direct access to Amazon.com pages (Movers & Shakers, Best Sellers, category pages) was blocked by the research environment's network egress proxy on all attempted fetches. All data was gathered from secondary sources: retail trade press, deal-tracking sites, product review publications, Amazon press releases via PR Newswire, brand official sites, and retail analytics coverage. This is noted as a degraded-mode fallback per the workflow's instructions — the degradation affected direct Amazon page access only, not overall research quality.

Verification rule: A product is marked VERIFIED when at least 2 independent sources corroborate its presence on Amazon's bestseller/trending lists or its current sales momentum. Amazon's product listing page counts as one source; a second editorial, retail-analytics, or deal-tracking source is required. Three products are marked LOW-CONFIDENCE where this bar was not met for today's date specifically (Keurig K-Mini Mate, Core Power, INABA Churu), though all three have strong historical presence in this report.

Data caveat — creatine figures: The figures cited in the Nutricost card ($57.5M/month, $721M trailing 12 months, 90% YoY surge, 620,729 monthly Amazon searches) appeared in a search result snippet from northwestsignal.net, which in turn cited a SmartScout analysis. Direct page access to northwestsignal.net and smartscout.com was blocked. These figures were read from the search engine snippet, not from the fetched article text, and should be treated as a secondary-source citation pending direct verification.
