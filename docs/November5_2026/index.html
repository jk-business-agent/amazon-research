Methodology & Sources

Source types checked: WebSearch was the primary discovery and corroboration tool for all 18 products. Direct WebFetch was attempted for major Amazon category pages (Movers & Shakers, Best Sellers, New Releases) and primary press sources; most attempts failed with ENOTFOUND DNS errors indicating network access restrictions in this cloud session. Amazon.com and many major press domains (TechRadar, Tom's Guide, CNN, NBC News, Yahoo Shopping, Today.com, About Amazon, AMZScout, ExplodingTopics, Hip2Save, ToyBook, and others) were outside this session's allowed network. All product data was sourced from WebSearch result snippets and the accessible subset of secondary-press and analytics domains.

Verification rule: VERIFIED = at least 2 independent sources corroborate the product's sales performance or trend driver. For this session, "source" means a WebSearch snippet from a named publication or analytics provider that specifically names the product in a sales/trend context. 13 of 18 products are VERIFIED; 5 are marked LOW-CONFIDENCE because corroborating sources for those specific products were either inaccessible via fetch or only one source could be confirmed.

Degraded-mode fallbacks used today: (1) Amazon Movers & Shakers, Best Sellers, and New Releases pages were inaccessible — all Amazon.com direct page fetches returned ENOTFOUND. Discovery relied entirely on secondary press and retail analytics coverage. (2) Multiple primary press domains (TechRadar, CNN Underscored, Tom's Guide, About Amazon) were cited in search snippets but could not be directly fetched to verify exact numbers — no specific figures from those sources were included in product cards unless the snippet text was sufficiently specific. Per workflow anti-fabrication rules, no sales figures, rank numbers, or revenue data were invented.

Anti-fabrication compliance: All specific figures in this report (prices, revenue estimates, unit counts) were sourced from WebSearch snippets that named specific amounts — e.g., AMZScout's cited $17.5M/month for AirPods Pro 3, $2.1M/month for Mighty Patch, 100K monthly units for CeraVe. Where prices are described as "approximately," the value was present in snippet text but could not be re-verified by direct page fetch. Where "price not confirmed" appears, no price figure was available in fetched or snippet content.
